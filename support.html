Support

Email support@code-it.info. Every request gets a reply from the person who builds ReleaseGuard.

What to include

  • Your Salesforce org ID (Setup → Company Information) and whether it is a production org or a sandbox.
  • The ReleaseGuard version (Setup → Installed Packages).
  • The rule code (for example RG-004) and what you expected to see.
  • Screenshots if they help. Please do not send passwords, security tokens or customer data.

Support hours and response times

Business days are Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00 Central European Time, excluding Polish public holidays. The response time is the time until the first reply from a person, not until the issue is resolved.

Security issues

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in ReleaseGuard, email support@code-it.info with the subject "Security report". Security reports are handled first, whatever your plan.

Rule requests

Know of a Salesforce retirement or enforcement ReleaseGuard should check? Send the link to the Salesforce release note or article. Rules are added only for documented platform changes.
